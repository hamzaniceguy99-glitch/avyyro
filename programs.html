The programs, in detail

Four plans from 8 to 20 weeks, each written around your fitness, your goal and the days you can actually run.

Program 01 · Couch to finish line

Your first 5K

The most common reason new runners quit is starting too fast. This plan is deliberately gentle: three short sessions a week, mostly walking at first, building to continuous running by week eight. Check with your doctor before starting if you have any health concerns.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginner
  • 3 sessions a week
  • Any shoes to start

Start this program

Weeks 1–3 · Walk-run

Short intervals, more walking than running, on purpose.

Weeks 4–6 · More running

Intervals lengthen while the effort stays easy.

Weeks 7–8 · Continuous

Twenty to thirty minutes without stopping.

Weeks 9–10 · Race week

A 5K, and a plan for what comes after it.

Phase 1 · Base

Easy mileage at conversation pace, plus strides.

Phase 2 · Quality

Intervals and tempo work, one session a week.

Phase 3 · Specific

Race-pace work and the longest runs.

Phase 4 · Taper

Less volume, fresh legs, a pacing plan for race day.

Program 02 · Go longer, stay healthy

10K & half marathon

The classic mistake at this level is running every run at the same medium-hard pace. We set your paces from a recent effort, keep easy days easy, and add one quality session a week. The long run grows on a schedule that respects how tissue adapts.

  • 12 to 16 weeks
  • Can run 20 min
  • 4 runs a week
  • A watch helps

Start this program

Program 03 · Twenty weeks, done right

Marathon

A marathon exposes everything you skipped. We build the long run gradually, rehearse fueling on every one of them, and practice goal pace when you're tired. The plan assumes life happens and includes rules for missed weeks.

  • 20 weeks
  • Half marathon done
  • 5 runs a week
  • Race entered

Start this program

Weeks 1–6 · Base

Volume and easy running, strength twice a week.

Weeks 7–12 · Build

Longer long runs, tempo work, fueling rehearsed.

Weeks 13–17 · Specific

Goal-pace segments inside long runs.

Weeks 18–20 · Taper

Cutting volume, keeping sharpness, race plan written.

Weeks 1–2 · Assessment

Video of your gait and a look at your current load.

Weeks 3–4 · Cadence

Small changes to turnover, practiced in short bouts.

Weeks 5–6 · Strength

A twenty-minute routine, twice a week, that you'll keep.

Weeks 7–8 · Building safely

Rules for adding mileage without spikes.

Program 04 · Run more, break less

Form & strength

Most running injuries come from doing too much too soon rather than from bad form, so we manage load first and tidy up form second. Coaching is not medical treatment: if you're in pain or injured, see a physician or physical therapist, and we'll work around their guidance.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 sessions a week
  • Nothing to buy
